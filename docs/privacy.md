# stoQr Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 30 October 2026 · **Version:** 2026-10-30

## Who we are

stoQr is run by Adewale Orotayo ("we", "us"), a sole trader based in the United Kingdom. We're responsible for your personal data under UK data protection law.

**Contact:** stoqr.app.team@gmail.com

## What stoQr is for

stoQr helps you keep track of what's in your cupboards, reminds you before you run out, and, if you choose, shows average prices that other stoQr shoppers have shared.

## What we collect

**When you create an account**

- Your email address and name
- Your password. It's stored securely by our database provider; we never see it.

**When you set up and use the app**

- The first half of your postcode (for example, SW16). We never store your full postcode or address.
- The shops you usually use
- The items in your cupboard: names, quantities, units, pack sizes, prices you've entered, shops, and the level at which you want a warning
- Your restocks: what you bought, how many, the price you paid, the shop, and the date
- Whether you've chosen to share your prices, when you chose, and which version of this policy applied at the time
- The date you finished setting up

**Kept only on your phone (never sent to us)**

- Items you add to your shopping list yourself
- Your notification setting. Low-stock alerts are created on your phone; we don't send push notifications and don't receive a notification token.

**What we don't collect**

We don't collect your precise location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers, or information about how you use the app. stoQr contains no analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

## Why we use it, and our legal basis

| What we do | Legal basis |
|---|---|
| Run your account and the app: your cupboard, alerts, shopping list, restocks, budget | **Contract.** We need it to provide the service you signed up for. |
| Include your prices in shared averages | **Your consent.** Off unless you turn it on. |
| Keep the service secure, prevent misuse and fix problems | **Legitimate interests** in running a safe, reliable service |
| Meet legal obligations, for example responding to lawful requests | **Legal obligation** |

## Price sharing

If you turn on **Share my prices** (during setup or in Profile):

- the prices you've paid in the last **90 days** are included in **average prices** other stoQr shoppers see;
- an average for an item at a shop is only shown once **at least 5 different shoppers** have shared a price for it;
- we only show averages: never individual prices, your name, your postcode or anything that identifies you;
- prices are compared per single item (per roll, per bottle) and are currently averaged across the UK.

You can turn sharing off at any time in **Profile**. From that moment your prices are no longer included in any average. Averages that other people saw before you turned it off can't be taken back from them. With sharing off, you can still use every other part of stoQr.

## Who we share data with

We don't sell your data and don't share it for advertising. We use these service providers, who process data only on our instructions:

- **Supabase** (database, sign-in and hosting). Your data is stored in **London, UK** (Amazon Web Services, eu-west-2).
- **stoqr.app.team@gmail.com** (sending account emails such as password resets)

Supabase is a US company. Where any of your data may be accessed from outside the UK, for example by their support staff, this is covered by safeguards approved under UK law. [Confirm with your reviewer that Supabase's data processing agreement covers UK transfers, and name the safeguard.]

Apple and Google handle downloads under their own privacy policies. We may disclose data if the law requires us to.

## How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account. When you delete your account (**Profile → Delete account**), your account, cupboard, purchase history, consent records and profile are deleted immediately from our database. Our providers' system logs, which can include your email address, are kept for a short period and then deleted automatically.

## Your rights

You have the right to:

- **access** the personal data we hold about you;
- **correct** it. You can edit most of it in the app.
- **delete** it. Delete your account in Profile, or ask us.
- **restrict** or **object to** how we use it;
- **get a copy** to take elsewhere (data portability);
- **withdraw consent** to price sharing at any time.

To use any of these rights, email stoqr.app.team@gmail.com. We'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, please tell us first. You can also complain to the **Information Commissioner's Office**: ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113.

## Age

stoQr is for people aged **18 and over**. If we learn that someone under 18 has an account, we'll delete it.

## Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Each person's data is separated in our database so that only they can access it, and passwords are stored by our sign-in provider using one-way hashing.

## Changes to this policy

If we make an important change, for example to how price sharing works, we'll tell you in the app and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent again. The date and version at the top show when this policy last changed.
